This is the whole product in one sitting: **capture → distill → search → retain**. By
the end you'll have written a journal entry, made a linked note, found it by search, and
turned it into a review card.

First, get the app running — see [Install & run](/start/install-and-run/) — then launch
it with `just dev-desktop`.

## Capture in the journal

<Steps>

1. Click **Journal** in the sidebar (or press **⌘K** → *Go to Journal*).

2. Type a line: `Learned about spaced repetition today`. Press **Enter** and add
   `It schedules reviews so I forget less`.

3. There's no save button — watch for the **Saved** indicator. Your daily note is
   persisted.

</Steps>

## Distill into a linked note

<Steps>

1. Open **Notes** → **New note**.

2. Title it by typing the first line: `Spaced repetition`. Add a sentence in your own
   words.

3. Go back to the **Journal** entry and link to it: type `[[Spaced repetition]]` in the
   journal.

4. Open the **Spaced repetition** note — it now shows your journal entry in the
   **Backlinks** pane.

</Steps>

## Find it by search

<Steps>

1. Press **⌘K** (Ctrl+K).

2. Type `repetition`. Your journal line and the note both appear.

3. Press **Enter** on a result to jump straight to it.

</Steps>

## Turn it into a review card

<Steps>

1. On the **Spaced repetition** note, choose **Add to review**.

2. Open **Review** (sidebar or **⌘K** → *Go to Review*).

3. Recall the idea, then grade yourself **Good**. FSRS schedules the next review.

</Steps>

## You've done the whole loop

You captured in the journal, distilled into a linked atomic note, found it with local
search, and started retaining it with spaced repetition — the entire *capture → distill
→ retain* axis.
