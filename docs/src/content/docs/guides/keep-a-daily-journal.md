The journal is the **capture** end of the workflow: a daily note, keyed by date and
created automatically, that's always one keystroke away. Get the thought down first;
[distill it into atomic notes](/guides/write-and-link-atomic-notes/) later.

See [How the system works](/how-it-works/system-overview/) for what happens to a
journal entry under the hood.
